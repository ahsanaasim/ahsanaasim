## 🤖 Agentic AI & How I Build in 2026

I treat AI agents as teammates, not toys. My teams and I build:

- **Autonomous & semi-autonomous AI agents** for coding, QA, data processing, and operations
- **LLM-powered product features**: RAG, tool use, structured extraction, and multi-agent orchestration
- **AI-accelerated SDLC**: agent-assisted planning, code generation, code review, test automation, and deployment
- **AI build systems** that take a product from requirements to a running app quickly, with humans in the loop where it matters

**Principle:** use AI to remove toil and raise the bar on quality, while keeping engineering judgment, security, and ownership with people.

## 💡 Leadership Principles

1. **Simplicity scales.** The best architecture is the one your team can understand, run, and change.
2. **Outcomes over output.** We measure impact on customers and the business, not lines of code.
3. **AI-first, human-accountable.** Agents do the heavy lifting; engineers own the decisions.
4. **Grow people, then products.** Strong teams build great products — mentoring is part of the job.
5. **Ship, learn, repeat.** Short feedback loops beat long plans.
